
# FEMCURA Research Passage Labeling Guide

## 1. Policy Discussion
- yes: The passage discusses or recommends policies, guidelines, or actions related to sex/gender inclusion or representation in research.
- no: The passage clearly does not discuss these topics.
- unclear: The passage lacks enough context to decide.

## 2. Study Reporting — Option A
- yes: The passage reports evidence from the current paper's own participants, methods, data, or results.
- no: The passage clearly falls outside the current paper's study-reporting scope.
- unclear: The passage does not provide enough evidence to decide.

## Important Rules
1. Findings from cited studies do not count as the current paper's own results.
2. A reference list is not evidence of original study reporting.
3. Missing evidence in one passage does not prove that the entire paper did not report it.
4. Never treat heuristic or AI-generated labels as manually verified.
5. Keep unclear labels when evidence is insufficient.
